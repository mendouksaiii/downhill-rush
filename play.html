      DESCENT CONTRACTS

      RESETS DAILY · DAY --
      Three objectives every day. Progress carries across runs, so chip away at them while you ride — clear all three for a bonus set.
      HOW THEY SCOREContracts pay coins, not points. Your leaderboard standing only moves when you beat your own best run.
      THEY STACKObjectives track in the background on every descent. You never have to pick one — just ride.
      SPEND ITCoins buy riders and bikes in the market. Skins are cosmetic and never change how the bike handles.

      HOW TO PLAY

      ENDLESS DOWNHILL · SURVIVE THE EYE
      You are always moving downhill. Speed builds on its own when you ride clean — your job is to stay alive long enough to let it.

      THE TWO MODES

      DESCENTEndless procedural mountain. Build speed, survive, and post the longest clean run you can. This is the mode that feeds the leaderboard.
      ASCENTAuthored climbs with checkpoints and a summit. Fixed maps, so it is about learning the route rather than reacting. Does not affect your rank.
